Privacy Policy

Our commitment to responsible information handling and user privacy.

Policy status: This policy is an initial website privacy notice. It must be reviewed and updated before introducing user registration, student records, online examinations, payment facilities or other services that collect personal information.
Website: Global Learning Hub
Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

Global Learning Hub aims to provide educational information and services for students, parents, tutors, educators and visitors. We respect the privacy of individuals who visit our website. This policy explains the information-handling practices relevant to the current website and the practices that must be established when additional services are introduced.

2. Information We May Collect

The current website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not currently provide a website-based user registration system, student database or online payment facility.

Depending on the features introduced in the future, the following categories of information may be collected:

  • Basic details such as name, email address and contact number.
  • Student details such as academic level, course and enrolment information.
  • Tutor details such as qualifications, teaching experience and subject specialisation.
  • Educational information such as assessments, marks and learning progress.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and website usage data processed by hosting or service providers.
  • Messages, enquiries, suggestions and feedback voluntarily submitted by users.

3. How Information May Be Used

If additional educational services are introduced, information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and managing educational services.
  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Managing courses, tutoring and examinations.
  • Communicating academic and service-related updates.
  • Responding to enquiries and providing support.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse.

4. Contact Enquiries and Information Sharing

The current contact form is designed to open the visitor's email application. It does not directly submit information to a website database or server. If a visitor chooses to send an email, the information in that email is sent through the visitor's email service to the intended recipient.

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. If future services require information sharing with hosting providers, technology partners or other service providers, the relevant purposes and safeguards should be explained in an updated policy.

5. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards when personal information is collected through future services. Access to confidential student, tutor and academic records should be restricted to authorised individuals according to their responsibilities.

No website, online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

If personal information is collected through future services, it should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, academic and administrative requirements. Procedures for secure deletion or anonymisation should be established.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website is currently a static website. Additional technologies such as analytics, advertising tools, login services or preference storage may be introduced in the future. If these technologies are used, this policy should be updated to explain what information they process and what choices are available to visitors.

GitHub Pages and other infrastructure providers may process technical information as part of hosting and delivering the website. Visitors can review the relevant provider's privacy information for details of its practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Educational services may be intended for learners under 18 years of age. Before collecting or processing children's personal information through future services, appropriate parental or guardian consent and other safeguards must be implemented in accordance with applicable laws.

9. User Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information and to raise concerns about how their information is handled. A suitable process for receiving and responding to such requests must be established before personal information is collected through future platform services.

10. External Links and Third-Party Services

This website may contain links to external websites, educational resources and third-party services. These websites and services operate independently and have their own privacy practices. Users should review the relevant privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Global Learning Hub develops and introduces new educational services or technologies. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date whenever changes are made.

12. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

For questions or concerns about this policy, please visit our Contact Us page. Official privacy contact details and a dedicated process for privacy requests should be established before the platform begins collecting personal information through additional services.

Important reminder: This page is a general website privacy notice, not a legal compliance certification. It should be reviewed against the actual services, data practices and applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements before the platform launches account-based services.
