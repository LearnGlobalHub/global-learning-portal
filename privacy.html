Privacy Policy

Our commitment to responsible information handling and user privacy.

Important: This is an initial privacy policy template for the website's planning stage. It must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual services, data collection practices, technology providers and applicable laws before collecting personal information or launching user accounts.

Website: Global Learning Hub

Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

Global Learning Hub aims to provide an educational platform for students, parents, tutors, educators and visitors. We respect the privacy of individuals who visit or use our website. This policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, protected and managed when the relevant services are introduced.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services implemented, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Basic details such as name, email address and contact number.
  • Student details such as academic level, course and enrolment information.
  • Tutor details such as qualifications, teaching experience and subject specialisation.
  • Educational information such as assessments, marks and learning progress.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and website usage data.
  • Messages, enquiries, suggestions and feedback voluntarily submitted by users.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate educational and operational purposes, including:

  • Providing and managing educational services.
  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Managing courses, tutoring and examinations.
  • Communicating important academic and service-related updates.
  • Responding to enquiries and providing technical support.
  • Improving website performance, accessibility and user experience.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with authorised service providers when necessary to operate the platform, provide requested services, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Appropriate safeguards and access restrictions should be established before such sharing takes place.

5. Data Security

We aim to implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. Access to confidential academic and personal records should be restricted to authorised individuals according to their responsibilities.

No website or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, academic and administrative requirements. Appropriate procedures should be established for secure deletion or anonymisation.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if these are introduced to support essential functions, remember preferences, improve performance or understand website usage. Details about actual cookies and available controls must be provided before such technologies are used.

8. Children's Privacy

Educational services may be used by learners who are under 18 years of age. Before collecting or processing children's personal information, appropriate parental or guardian consent and other safeguards must be implemented in accordance with applicable laws.

9. User Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, and to raise concerns about how their information is handled. Requests will be managed through the designated privacy contact process once established.

10. External Links and Services

The website may contain links to external websites and third-party educational services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Users should review those policies before sharing personal information with external services.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website develops and new educational services are introduced. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

For questions or concerns about this policy, please visit our Contact Us page. Official privacy contact details should be added before the platform begins collecting personal information.
